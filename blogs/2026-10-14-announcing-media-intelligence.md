Webex meetings generate valuable audio, video, and conversational data. If you build a product, integration, or workflow around meetings, Media Intelligence gives you a way to bring Webex meeting media into that experience. As a developer or ISV, you can connect your application to Webex, apply the AI, analytics, and processing services that fit your product, and deliver capabilities tailored to your customers and industry.
