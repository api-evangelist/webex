Administrators can now configure group-level access for Webex MCP servers connected to external AI agents and clients, such as Claude Desktop, Cursor, and VS Code. In Webex Control Hub, Limited access lets you choose which groups discover a server's enabled functions, with one shared audience for all functions or different groups for individual tools, resources, and prompts. For connection setup and authentication, follow Connect Webex MCP Servers to External Clients .
